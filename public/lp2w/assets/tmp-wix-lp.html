Frequently Asked Questions

Please feel free to contact us for further queries.

  • How can you help me with Wix?

    Wix Profs is a dedicated service for creating and managing Wix websites. We offer everything from site design and setup to ongoing support, focusing on delivering a website that reflects your brand and helps you achieve your business goals.

  • Do I need any technical skills to understand website process?

    Not at all! We handle all the technical details so you don’t have to. Our team will take care of the design, setup, and maintenance, and we’ll keep you informed in simple terms throughout the process.

  • How long does it take to build a Wix website?

    It typically takes 2–4 weeks, depending on the complexity of your project and your specific needs. We’ll provide a detailed timeline after discussing your requirements, so you know exactly what to expect.

  • Can I update my Wix website after it’s built?

    Absolutely! Wix is user-friendly, and we can show you how to make basic updates. If you’d prefer ongoing help, we also offer support packages to handle updates and changes for you.

  • What if I need extra features on my website?

    No problem! We offer custom API integration services, which means we can add specialized features like payment gateways, booking systems, or other third-party tools to enhance your site’s functionality.

  • Will my website be optimized for mobile devices?

    Yes, we make sure your website is fully responsive, meaning it will look great on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops. This ensures a smooth experience for visitors no matter how they access your site.

  • Do you offer eCommerce solutions for Wix websites?

    Yes, we specialize in building and optimizing eCommerce stores on Wix. From product pages to secure checkout, we’ll set up everything you need to start selling online with ease.

  • What kind of support do you provide after my website is live?

    We offer ongoing support and maintenance options to keep your site running smoothly. Whether you need updates, troubleshooting, or just have a question, we’re here to help even after launch.

  • Is there a money-back guarantee if I’m not satisfied?

    Yes, we stand behind our work with a money-back guarantee. If you’re not happy with the results, we’ll work with you to make it right or provide a full refund. Your satisfaction is our priority.
